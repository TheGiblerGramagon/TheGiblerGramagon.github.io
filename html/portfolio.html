Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for Chess Piece Classification

    I used PyTorch to design and train a CNN to classify chess pieces. At the end of training the CNN was able to identify the piece with 97% accuracy in testing. To train the model, I built my own dataset and downloaded datasets online. To create my own photos, I took ~150 photos of a chessboard from different angles and different positions. For each photo I manually labeled the corners, computed a homography to normalize the squares. From there, an algorithm identified each square (using the corners) and labeled the piece using a string that represented the board position. This created 150 * 64 squares ~10,000 images. The images are primarily empty squares, so I pruned most of the empty squares to balance the dataset. I initially attempted to identify every piece individually, but my CNN was not accurate enough, so I simplified the model to classify squares as white, empty, or black, the minimum required information to track a game. I never achieved the accuracy I was looking for, but the project was an amazing learning experience.
